Skew-T Sounding Dashboard

Data: Iowa Environmental Mesonet RAOB archive (NWS radiosondes) for the station you pick (default KXMR, Cape Canaveral). For each date you add, every sounding launched within your chosen hour range, in the selected time zone, is plotted, including non-synoptic launches. Leave the range at 0–24 for all soundings that day. Thermal levels and indices are computed in your browser from the sounding (virtual-temperature parcel method, pseudo-adiabatic ascent), so values can differ somewhat from SHARPpy or SPC output. Hover a table row to see that level or parameter drawn on the skew-T. Red = temperature, green = dewpoint, dashed orange = dry adiabats.
